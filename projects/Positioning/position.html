Wright Brothers first Plane

This first plane had a 12-horsepower engine running at 1,200 rpm and weighing 170 pounds. It also had two propellers designed by the Wrights that exceeded their theoretical calculations.

After the first flight, the Wright brothers conducted further experiments in Dayton. By the end of 1904, they could stay in the air for five minutes and in 1905, they sustained the flight for over 24 miles. Therefore Aviation was Introducted the to world changing travel time
