My Hero-Stanislav Petrov

The hero in the story which is a True Story is Stanislaw Petrow a Lieutenant Colonel in the Soviet Air Defense Forces who in 1983 correctly identified A Satellite warning of U.S missile launches as a false alarm, preventing a catastrophic retaliatory strike by trusting his gut over faulty COMPUTER systems. His decision on September 26, 1983, averted what could have escalated into globel,nuclear conflict during th height of the Cold War.

The Event
The Oko satellite system reported multiple U.S. intercontinental ballistic missiles launched the Soviet Union.
Petrov's Action
He reasoned that a real first strike would involve hundreds of missiles, not just a few, but reported back it was system malfunction rather than a genuine attack
Outcome
His jugdement proved correct, preventing a Soviet counter-attack and potential nuclear Armageddon
