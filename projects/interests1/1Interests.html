Weight Brothers Eva

The Wright brothers achieved the world's first controlled, sustained flight of a powered, heavier-than-air aircraft on December 17, 1903. Orville Wright piloted the inaugural 12-second flight, which covered 120 feet, at Kill Devil Hills, near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina. They made four flights that day, with the longest lasting 59 seconds.

Boeing 747

The Boeing 747, is famously known as the "Queen of the Skies," an iconic long-range, wide-body airliner known for its distinct hump-shaped upper deck. Introduced in 1970 as the first twin-aisle "Jumbo Jet," it revolutionized air travel, offering high-capacity, long-distance flights with seating for over 400–600 passengers depending on configuration.

Boeing 737

The Boeing 737 is highly popular, American-made, narrow-body airliner first introduced in 1968, with over 12,370 built as of late 2025. Known for its versatility, it serves short-to-medium routes with a typical seating capacity of 85 to 215 passengers. The latest generation, the 737 MAX, offers improved fuel efficiency.
