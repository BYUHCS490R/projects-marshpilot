Wake Time

  1. Wake up and take a moment to stretch and fully open your eyes.
  2. Sit up, check your phone for any important messages or notifications.
  3. Head to the bathroom to wash your face and get refreshed.
  4. Walk to the kitchen and prepare a simple breakfast like eggs, toast, cereal, or whatever you prefer.
  5. Sit down and eat your breakfast calmly while thinking about the day ahead.
  6. Clean up your dishes and tidy the kitchen area.
  7. Get dressed and ready for the rest of your morning tasks.

Classes Time

  1. Check the time after breakfast to make sure you’re on schedule for class.
  2. Gather your essentials — backpack, laptop, notebooks, pens, and any assignments due.
  3. to your class location or log in if it’s an online session.
  4. a few minutes early so you can settle in and get organized.
  5. Review your notes or the previous lesson while waiting for class to begin.
  6. Attend class, listen carefully, participate when needed, and take clear notes.
  7. After class ends, check for homework, announcements, or upcoming deadlines before leaving.
